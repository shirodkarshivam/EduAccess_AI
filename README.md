# 🎓 EduAccess AI
> **Tagline:** *"Making Education Accessible for Every Student"*

EduAccess AI is an AI-powered educational accessibility platform designed to help students with disabilities learn effectively. It breaks down learning barriers using Artificial Intelligence, universal web accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA), and interactive assistive tools.

---

## 🚀 Pitch Overview (The Problem & Solution)

### 🔴 The Problem
Over **1 Billion people worldwide** experience disabilities. In classrooms, many students struggle because:
- **Deaf / Hard-of-Hearing Students** cannot follow spoken teacher lectures.
- **Visually Impaired Students** cannot see textbook diagrams or graphs.
- **Dyslexic & Neurodivergent Students** experience reading fatigue from standard fonts.
- **Non-Native Language Learners** face language barriers in study materials.

### 🟢 The Solution
**EduAccess AI** brings 6 essential AI accessibility tools into one clean, fast, and easy-to-use website:

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🎙️ Speech-to-Text (Hearing Support)
- **What it does:** Listens to teacher speech and creates live captions on screen in real time.
- **Who it helps:** Deaf and hard-of-hearing students.

### 2. 🔊 Text-to-Speech (Visual & Reading Support)
- **What it does:** Reads study materials out loud while highlighting sentences line-by-line.
- **Who it helps:** Visually impaired students and dyscalculic learners.

### 3. 👁️ AI Image Description (Visual Impairment)
- **What it does:** Uses **Google Gemini Vision AI** to examine complex diagrams, charts, and science illustrations, describing them clearly for blind students.
- **Who it helps:** Visually impaired and blind students.

### 4. 📄 OCR Text Reader (Optical Character Recognition)
- **What it does:** Uses **Tesseract.js OCR** to extract printed text from textbook photos and read it out loud.
- **Who it helps:** Students who prefer listening to physical books.

### 5. 📖 Dyslexia Reading Mode (Neurodiversity Support)
- **What it does:** Instantly adjusts fonts (Atkinson Hyperlegible), applies a warm cream background, and customizes font size, line height, and letter spacing.
- **Who it helps:** Students with dyslexia and ADHD.

### 6. 🌐 AI Translation (Language Accessibility)
- **What it does:** Translates educational text across 8 regional and global languages (Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Arabic) with native voice playback.
- **Who it helps:** Non-native English speakers and multilingual learners.

### ♿ Accessibility Quick Suite (Floating Toolbar)
- Dark Mode toggle
- High Contrast Mode
- Instant Font Scaler (`-A` / `Normal` / `+A`)
- Screen Reader simulation

## 💡 Pitch Talking Points (Why EduAccess AI Stands Out)

1. **100% Free & Accessible:** Runs directly in any modern web browser without installing heavy software.
2. **WCAG 2.1 Level AA Compliant:** Built with high-contrast colors, keyboard navigation, and screen reader friendliness.
3. **Real-Time AI Powered:** Powered by **Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision** and **Tesseract OCR**.
4. **Privacy-First Design:** Processes data in the browser whenever possible.

## 💻 How to Run & Present the Demo

1. Open the project folder on your computer.
2. Double-click **`index.html`** or open **`http://localhost:8080`** in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
3. Scroll through the interactive cards during your pitch to demonstrate:
   - Click **"Start Microphone Demo"** under Speech-to-Text.
   - Click **"Read"** under Text-to-Speech to see live sentence highlighting.
   - Upload any textbook photo into **OCR Text Reader** to see real-time character recognition.
   - Toggle **Dyslexia Mode** to show the instant font and spacing shift.
